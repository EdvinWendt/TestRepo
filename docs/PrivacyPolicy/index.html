Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to Kvitt, a receipt-sharing and payment-request app. It explains what information Kvitt accesses, collects, uses, stores, and shares when you use the app and related payment-link pages.

1. Who This Policy Applies To

This Privacy Policy applies to Kvitt and its related payment-link website hosted at edvinwendt.github.io/TestRepo. In this policy, "Kvitt," "we," "us," and "our" refer to the Kvitt app and its developer.

2. Information We Access or Collect

  • Account information: email address, password processed through Supabase Auth, username or display name, and phone number.
  • Contacts information: contact names and phone numbers if you grant contacts permission, including your device profile phone number when used for account setup.
  • Receipt information: receipt photos or imported files, OCR-extracted text, item names, prices, payer and participant selections, totals, and related receipt details.
  • Payment-request information: recipient phone numbers, payment amounts, request names, custom messages, payment card IDs, payment status, and history identifiers.
  • App and device data: app settings, favorites, pre-added participants, local archives, login session tokens, and network-state information needed for app features.

3. How We Use Information

  • To create, authenticate, and manage your Kvitt account.
  • To let you scan receipts, split costs, manage participants, and calculate payment shares.
  • To load phone contacts and phone numbers when you choose to use contact-based features.
  • To send SMS payment reminders from your device.
  • To store and sync profile details and receipt history with Supabase.
  • To provide payment-link pages that resolve payment details from Supabase and open Swish.
  • To maintain app functionality, reliability, and security.

4. Permissions We Request

  • Camera: used to photograph receipts for scanning.
  • Contacts: used to load phone contacts and, where supported, your device profile phone number.
  • SMS: used to send payment-request text messages from your device.
  • Internet and network state: used to authenticate with Supabase, sync data, and detect online or offline status.

5. How Information Is Stored

  • On your device: Kvitt stores app settings, local archive data, favorites, pre-added participants, and session tokens in app-local storage.
  • In Supabase: Kvitt stores account-related profile data and synced receipt-history data in a Supabase backend.
  • On-device processing: receipt scanning is designed to use on-device text recognition for OCR, although receipt-derived results may later be stored locally or synced to Supabase.

6. Sharing and Third Parties

Kvitt does not sell your personal information.

  • Supabase: used for authentication, account/profile metadata, receipt history, and payment-link lookups.
  • Device and platform providers: the app interacts with Android system services for camera access, contacts access, and SMS sending.
  • GitHub Pages website flow: payment links may open a GitHub Pages-hosted page that uses identifiers in the link to retrieve payment details from Supabase before opening Swish.

Based on the current app code, Kvitt does not include third-party advertising SDKs and does not appear to use a separate analytics SDK.

7. Data Retention

  • Receipt history in Supabase: designed to be retained for up to 30 days before automatic deletion.
  • Account deletion: if you use the in-app Delete Account option, Kvitt is designed to delete your authenticated Supabase account and associated backend records linked to that account sooner than the normal retention period.
  • Local app data: archives, settings, favorites, and other locally stored information may remain on your device until you remove them in the app or uninstall the app.
  • Account/profile information: may remain associated with your account until updated or deleted.

8. Your Choices

  • You can choose not to grant camera, contacts, or SMS permissions, but some features will not work without them.
  • You can edit your username and phone number in the app.
  • You can sign out of the app at any time.
  • You can use the in-app Delete Account option to request deletion of your authenticated Kvitt account.
  • You can remove some data inside the app, such as archive items or history entries, where those controls are available.

9. Data Deletion Requests and Contact

Kvitt includes an in-app Delete Account option. When that option succeeds, the app is designed to delete your authenticated Supabase user account and associated backend data linked to that account, and it also clears certain local account data from the device.

Some local app preferences or other device-stored information that are not directly tied to the deleted account may remain on the device until you remove them manually or uninstall the app.

If you want to ask privacy-related questions or need help with deletion, please contact the developer through GitHub Issues or the developer contact details listed for Kvitt on Google Play once the app is published.

Please do not post passwords or other sensitive information in a public GitHub issue.

10. Security

Kvitt relies on Android permission controls, authenticated Supabase access, and app-local storage protections provided by the Android platform. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we aim to limit access to data to what is needed to provide app features.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

Important: Google Play still requires accurate Data safety disclosures in the Play Console. This page is the public privacy policy for Kvitt, but it does not by itself complete those separate Play Console requirements.
